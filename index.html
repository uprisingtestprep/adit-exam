ADIT Principles Timed Mock Paper Tool

Twelve full mock papers in the real layout (three essay questions in Part A, two scenario questions in Part B, answer four), a 3 hour 15 minute clock, and a model answer with marking points for every question. Enter the access code printed in your book.

Choose a mock paper or a single question

The real paper has five questions of 25 marks, answered four at a time (at least two from Part A, at least one from Part B) in 3 hours 15 minutes. This tool times you and shows the model answer and marking points; it cannot read your writing, so you mark yourself. The pass mark was not stated on the official pages consulted, so your result is compared with a practice benchmark of 50% that is this book's own guide, not an official cutoff.
